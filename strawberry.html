Bloody Strawberries

Fresh strawberries dipped in rich white and dark chocolate — spooky, sweet, and irresistible!

Ingredients

  • Fresh strawberries
  • White chocolate melts
  • Dark chocolate (for detailing)
  • Butter or coconut oil (for smooth coating)
  • Edible red syrup (optional for “blood” effect)
